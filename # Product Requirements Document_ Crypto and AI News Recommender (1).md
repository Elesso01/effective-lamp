**\# Product Requirements Document: Crypto and AI News Recommender**

**\#\# 1\. Product overview**

**The Crypto and AI News Recommender helps a broad audience stay up to date on important developments in cryptocurrency and artificial intelligence.**

**It does more than collect headlines. It selects relevant stories, explains why they matter, provides useful context, and helps readers discover related topics without overwhelming them.**

**The product should be informative, balanced, accessible, and clearly separate news and analysis from investment advice.**

**\#\# 2\. Product vision**

**Make it easy for anyone to understand the most important developments in crypto and AI in a few minutes each day.**

**\#\# 3\. Goals**

**\#\#\# Primary goals**

**\- Help readers stay informed about important crypto and AI developments**  
**\- Help readers quickly understand why a story matters**  
**\- Personalize recommendations without requiring extensive setup**  
**\- Encourage discovery beyond a reader’s existing interests**  
**\- Present complex topics in clear, accessible language**  
**\- Build trust through balanced coverage and transparent recommendations**

**\#\#\# Secondary goals**

**\- Help readers follow evolving topics over time**  
**\- Show connections between crypto and AI**  
**\- Support different levels of familiarity, from beginner to advanced reader**  
**\- Create a habit around a daily news briefing**

**\#\#\# Non-goals**

**\- Providing personalized investment advice**  
**\- Predicting cryptocurrency prices**  
**\- Acting as a trading platform**  
**\- Replacing professional financial, legal, or investment advice**  
**\- Covering every available story**  
**\- Optimizing exclusively for clicks or sensational headlines**

**\#\# 4\. Target audience**

**The primary audience is broad: people interested in crypto, AI, technology, business, and society.**

**They may include:**

**\- Curious newcomers**  
**\- Technology professionals**  
**\- Business leaders**  
**\- Startup founders**  
**\- Investors seeking general information**  
**\- Researchers and analysts**  
**\- Readers interested in regulation and social impact**

**The product should not assume expert knowledge. Stories should remain useful to informed readers while being understandable to newcomers.**

**\#\# 5\. Core product principles**

**1\. \*\*Useful before comprehensive\*\***    
   **Show the most relevant and important stories rather than the largest number of stories.**

**2\. \*\*Context before complexity\*\***    
   **Explain unfamiliar concepts and provide background where needed.**

**3\. \*\*Balanced over sensational\*\***    
   **Avoid recommendations driven only by controversy, fear, hype, or market excitement.**

**4\. \*\*Reader control with helpful discovery\*\***    
   **Readers choose their interests, while the product introduces related topics.**

**5\. \*\*News is not advice\*\***    
   **Clearly distinguish reporting, analysis, opinion, and promotional content.**

**6\. \*\*Trust through transparency\*\***    
   **Explain why a story was recommended and identify its source and publication time.**

**\#\# 6\. Product experience**

**\#\#\# 6.1 Daily briefing**

**The daily briefing is the default experience.**

**It should include:**

**\- A concise selection of the most important crypto stories**  
**\- A concise selection of the most important AI stories**  
**\- A small number of cross-topic stories**  
**\- A short explanation of why each story matters**  
**\- Links to the original source**  
**\- Optional additional reading for interested readers**

**The briefing should be short enough to read quickly, while allowing readers to explore further.**

**\#\#\# 6.2 Personalized feed**

**The personalized feed continuously recommends stories based on:**

**\- Selected interests**  
**\- Followed topics, organizations, and people**  
**\- Reading behavior**  
**\- Saved stories**  
**\- Hidden or dismissed topics**  
**\- Preferred level of explanation**  
**\- Interest in crypto, AI, or both**

**The feed should include a healthy mix of familiar and new topics.**

**\#\#\# 6.3 Breaking-news alerts**

**Breaking alerts should be optional and limited to genuinely significant developments, such as:**

**\- Major regulatory decisions**  
**\- Significant security incidents**  
**\- Important company or research announcements**  
**\- Major product launches**  
**\- Large-scale market or industry events**  
**\- Developments with broad social or economic impact**

**Readers should be able to turn alerts off easily.**

**\#\#\# 6.4 Topic exploration**

**Readers should be able to browse stories by broad categories.**

**Suggested categories:**

**\- Markets and business**  
**\- Regulation and policy**  
**\- Startups and companies**  
**\- Research and development**  
**\- Products and applications**  
**\- Security and privacy**  
**\- Society and culture**  
**\- Jobs and education**  
**\- Climate and energy**  
**\- Crypto and AI intersections**

**\#\#\# 6.5 Story formats**

**Each story should offer several levels of depth:**

**\- \*\*Quick summary:\*\* what happened**  
**\- \*\*Why it matters:\*\* likely significance and affected groups**  
**\- \*\*Background:\*\* relevant definitions and previous events**  
**\- \*\*Read more:\*\* original reporting and related stories**  
**\- \*\*Different perspectives:\*\* when the topic is disputed or politically sensitive**

**\#\# 7\. Reader onboarding**

**Onboarding should be brief and optional where possible.**

**Readers should be asked to select:**

**\#\#\# Topic interests**

**\- Crypto markets**  
**\- Blockchain technology**  
**\- Digital assets**  
**\- Regulation**  
**\- Web3 applications**  
**\- AI research**  
**\- Generative AI**  
**\- AI products**  
**\- AI companies**  
**\- AI policy and safety**  
**\- AI business impact**  
**\- The relationship between crypto and AI**

**\#\#\# Reader familiarity**

**\- New to the topic**  
**\- Some familiarity**  
**\- Very familiar**

**This should affect the level of explanation, not restrict access to stories.**

**\#\#\# Reading preferences**

**\- Daily briefing time**  
**\- Preferred briefing length**  
**\- Email, web, or mobile delivery**  
**\- Whether to receive breaking alerts**  
**\- Preferred balance between crypto and AI**

**\#\#\# Followed subjects**

**Readers may follow:**

**\- Companies**  
**\- Projects**  
**\- Researchers**  
**\- Executives**  
**\- Policymakers**  
**\- Regulations**  
**\- Products**  
**\- Technologies**  
**\- Specific themes**

**\#\# 8\. Recommendation behavior**

**The recommender should consider four main factors:**

**\#\#\# Relevance**

**Does the story match the reader’s selected interests or followed subjects?**

**\#\#\# Importance**

**Is the story significant enough to deserve the reader’s attention?**

**\#\#\# Timeliness**

**Is the story recent, developing, or still important because of its continuing impact?**

**\#\#\# Diversity**

**Does the recommendation broaden the reader’s perspective and avoid showing repetitive coverage?**

**The product should deliberately balance:**

**\- Personal relevance**  
**\- Major developments outside the reader’s usual interests**  
**\- Different perspectives**  
**\- Crypto and AI coverage**  
**\- News and explanatory content**  
**\- Familiar and emerging topics**

**\#\#\# Recommendation explanation**

**Each recommendation should include a simple reason, such as:**

**\- “Because you follow AI regulation”**  
**\- “Related to your interest in crypto security”**  
**\- “A major development affecting both AI and blockchain”**  
**\- “You read several stories about this company”**  
**\- “Popular among readers interested in AI startups”**

**\#\# 9\. Editorial standards**

**\#\#\# Source quality**

**The product should favor sources that demonstrate:**

**\- Clear authorship**  
**\- Evidence-based reporting**  
**\- Corrections and updates**  
**\- Relevant expertise**  
**\- Separation between editorial content and advertising**  
**\- A track record of accurate coverage**

**\#\#\# Story labeling**

**Stories should be clearly labeled as:**

**\- News**  
**\- Analysis**  
**\- Opinion**  
**\- Research**  
**\- Company announcement**  
**\- Sponsored or promotional content**  
**\- Correction or update**

**\#\#\# Multiple perspectives**

**For controversial issues, the product should provide relevant viewpoints and avoid presenting disputed claims as established facts.**

**\#\#\# Financial content**

**Financial and market stories should:**

**\- Avoid exaggerated predictions**  
**\- Identify uncertainty**  
**\- Distinguish facts from opinions**  
**\- Avoid direct buy, sell, or hold recommendations**  
**\- Include appropriate educational context**

**\#\#\# Corrections and updates**

**When information changes or a story is corrected, readers should be able to see that clearly.**

**\#\#\# Must-have features**

**\- Daily crypto and AI briefing**  
**\- Personalized news feed**  
**\- Topic and subject selection**  
**\- Follow and unfollow controls**  
**\- Save stories**  
**\- Hide stories or topics**  
**\- Breaking-news alert controls**  
**\- Story summaries**  
**\- “Why it matters” explanations**  
**\- Source attribution**  
**\- News category browsing**  
**\- Beginner-friendly explanations**  
**\- Clear labeling of news, analysis, opinion, and announcements**  
**\- Feedback controls such as “More like this” and “Less like this”**

**\#\#\# Should-have features**

**\- Related-story collections**  
**\- Topic timelines**  
**\- Company and project pages**  
**\- AI and crypto crossover section**  
**\- Weekly digest**  
**\- Reading history**  
**\- Multiple explanation levels**  
**\- “Catch me up” summaries for developing stories**  
**\- Comparison of different coverage of the same event**

**\#\#\# Could-have features**

**\- Personalized topic briefings**  
**\- Audio versions of briefings**  
**\- Expert-curated collections**  
**\- Reader discussion areas**  
**\- Multilingual summaries**  
**\- Custom newsletters**  
**\- Shared briefings**  
**\- Calendar of upcoming industry events**

**\#\# 11\. Primary user journeys**

**\#\#\# New reader**

**1\. Opens the product**  
**2\. Selects a few interests**  
**3\. Chooses a familiarity level**  
**4\. Receives a concise briefing**  
**5\. Reads a summary and explanation**  
**6\. Saves or dismisses stories**  
**7\. Returns the next day for an updated briefing**

**\#\#\# Returning reader**

**1\. Opens the daily briefing**  
**2\. Reviews the top stories**  
**3\. Reads a story of interest**  
**4\. Explores related coverage**  
**5\. Saves an important story**  
**6\. Receives recommendations adjusted to their interests**

**\#\#\# Reader discovering a new topic**

**1\. Sees a recommendation outside their usual interests**  
**2\. Reads a simple explanation**  
**3\. Chooses to follow the topic**  
**4\. Receives more relevant coverage over time**

**\#\#\# Reader avoiding unwanted content**

**1\. Dismisses a story**  
**2\. Selects a reason, such as “not interested” or “too repetitive”**  
**3\. Sees fewer similar recommendations**  
**4\. Can review and change preferences later**

**\#\# 12\. MVP scope**

**The first version should focus on building a reliable daily reading habit.**

**\#\#\# MVP contents**

**\- Simple onboarding**  
**\- Crypto and AI interest selection**  
**\- Daily briefing**  
**\- Personalized feed**  
**\- Story summaries**  
**\- “Why it matters” explanations**  
**\- Basic topic categories**  
**\- Follow, save, and hide actions**  
**\- Optional breaking alerts**  
**\- Source and content-type labels**  
**\- Feedback controls**  
**\- Basic weekly digest**

**\#\#\# Excluded from MVP**

**\- Community discussions**  
**\- Audio content**  
**\- Advanced social sharing**  
**\- Extensive company profiles**  
**\- Custom editorial collections**  
**\- Complex reader-to-reader features**  
**\- Investment recommendations**  
**\- Highly specialized expert tools**

**\#\# 13\. Success metrics**

**\#\#\# Engagement**

**\- Percentage of readers who return for multiple briefings**  
**\- Daily and weekly active readers**  
**\- Average number of stories read per briefing**  
**\- Percentage of readers who complete onboarding**  
**\- Percentage of readers who save or follow content**

**\#\#\# Recommendation quality**

**\- Reader ratings of recommendation usefulness**  
**\- “More like this” and “Less like this” feedback**  
**\- Story completion rate**  
**\- Percentage of readers who discover and follow a new topic**  
**\- Reduction in repeated or irrelevant recommendations**

**\#\#\# Trust and quality**

**\- Reader ratings for clarity and balance**  
**\- Source click-through rate**  
**\- Correction frequency**  
**\- Reports of misleading or promotional content**  
**\- Percentage of stories with appropriate labels**  
**\- Reader confidence that the product is not giving investment advice**

**\#\#\# Retention**

**\- Return rate after the first briefing**  
**\- One-week and one-month retention**  
**\- Number of readers who maintain a daily briefing habit**  
**\- Unsubscribe and notification opt-out rates**

**\#\# 14\. Risks and mitigations**

**| Risk | Mitigation |**  
**| :--- | :--- |**  
**| Sensational stories receive too much attention | Include importance and source-quality standards |**  
**| Readers feel overwhelmed | Use a concise default briefing and optional deeper coverage |**  
**| Recommendations become repetitive | Include discovery and diversity goals |**  
**| Financial content is interpreted as advice | Use clear labels, balanced language, and disclaimers |**  
**| Low-quality or promotional sources appear | Review source credibility and label announcements |**  
**| Complex stories are oversimplified | Provide context, background, and links to deeper coverage |**  
**| Political or regulatory topics become one-sided | Include multiple credible perspectives |**  
**| Breaking alerts create notification fatigue | Make alerts selective and fully optional |**  
**| Readers do not trust automated recommendations | Explain why each story was selected |**  
**| Crypto and AI audiences have different needs | Offer separate sections plus a dedicated crossover section |**

**\#\# 15\. Product tone and editorial voice**

**The product should be:**

**\- Clear**  
**\- Calm**  
**\- Curious**  
**\- Neutral**  
**\- Contextual**  
**\- Concise**  
**\- Accessible**  
**\- Honest about uncertainty**

**It should avoid:**

**\- Hype**  
**\- Fear-based language**  
**\- Overconfident predictions**  
**\- Excessive jargon**  
**\- Investment promises**  
**\- Clickbait headlines**  
**\- Unexplained technical claims**

**\#\# 16\. Recommended default experience**

**The best default experience is:**

**\- One daily briefing**  
**\- Five to eight key stories**  
**\- Balanced coverage of crypto and AI**  
**\- One or two discovery stories outside the reader’s usual interests**  
**\- A short “why it matters” explanation for every story**  
**\- Optional breaking alerts for major events**  
**\- A weekly digest for readers who want a broader review**

**This approach gives readers the convenience of staying current while preserving personalization, discovery, context, and trust.**
